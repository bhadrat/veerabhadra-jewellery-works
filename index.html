Gold Hallmark

Every gram, certified.

A BIS hallmark is the Government of India's guarantee of purity. Every hallmarked gold piece carries three marks, laser-engraved and recorded against a unique ID.

BIS Mark
22K916Purity
AB12C3HUID
Illustration · sample HUID
1

BIS Standard Mark

The Bureau of Indian Standards mark, confirming the piece was tested and certified at a BIS-recognised assaying & hallmarking centre.

2

Purity / Fineness Grade

Karat and fineness together, like 22K916: 91.6% pure gold. Other grades include 18K750 and 14K585.

3

HUID: Hallmark Unique ID

A 6-character alphanumeric code unique to each piece. Mandatory on hallmarked gold jewellery in India since July 2021.

🔍

Verify any HUID yourself

  1. Install the official BIS CARE app (Android / iOS).
  2. Tap "Verify HUID" and enter the 6-character code on your jewellery.
  3. See the jeweller, hallmarking centre, purity and article type.

Jewellery Care

Keep it shining.

💧

Avoid chemicals

Remove jewellery before using soap, perfume, hairspray or cleaning products.

🧼

Gentle cleaning

Warm water, mild soap and a soft brush, then dry with a soft cloth.

📦

Store separately

Keep each piece in its own soft pouch or box to avoid scratches and tangles.

🥈

Silver tarnish

Store silver in air-tight bags; a silver polishing cloth brings back the shine.

💎

Check settings

Get stone settings checked once a year so no stone goes missing.

✨

Professional polish

FAQ

Good to know.

What is HUID and why does it matter?

HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) is a 6-character alphanumeric code laser-marked on each hallmarked gold piece. It links the piece to its purity test and hallmarking centre, and you can verify it in the BIS CARE app.

What does 916 mean on gold?

916 is the fineness of 22 karat gold: 91.6% pure gold, with the rest made of alloy metals for strength. 750 is 18K and 585 is 14K.

Is your gold hallmarked?

Our gold jewellery is BIS hallmarked, and every piece is weighed in front of you. Ask us to show you the hallmark and HUID on any piece.

Can you make a design from a photo?

Yes. Send a photo or sketch on WhatsApp with your budget, and we'll suggest options, weight and a timeline.

Do you exchange or remake old gold?

Yes. Bring your old jewellery; we test and weigh it and can remake it into a new design or adjust its value against a new purchase.

How is the price of jewellery calculated?

Price = (net gold weight × day's rate for that purity) + making charges + stone charges (if any) + 3% GST. Use the calculator above for an estimate.
